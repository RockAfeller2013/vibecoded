# Uber for Nursing – USA

An overview of US healthcare staffing platforms that connect nurses and healthcare facilities through an on-demand shift marketplace.

## 1. Leading Platforms

### ShiftKey
- **Website:** https://www.shiftkey.com/
- Connects RNs, LPNs and CNAs with available shifts.
- Nurses can choose shifts and bid their rates.
- Strong presence in nursing homes and aged care.
- Offers next-day payment options.

### Clipboard Health
- **Website:** https://www.clipboardhealth.com/
- Marketplace connecting healthcare facilities with nurses.
- Nurses browse and book available shifts.
- Covers hospitals, nursing homes and aged care.
- Uses an independent contractor model.

## 3. Business Model

These platforms operate similarly to Uber:

1. Healthcare facilities publish available shifts.
2. Nurses register and complete credential verification.
3. Nurses browse and select available shifts.
4. Facilities confirm bookings.
5. Nurses attend and complete their shifts.
6. The platform manages attendance, payments and administrative processes.

## 4. Core Platform Capabilities

| Component | Function |
|---|---|
| Nurse Registration | Nurse profiles and onboarding |
| Credential Verification | Licences, certifications and background checks |
| Facility Management | Healthcare provider registration and management |
| Shift Marketplace | Publish and browse available shifts |
| Booking Engine | Shift applications, confirmations and cancellations |
| Scheduling | Nurse availability and shift calendars |
| Notifications | Booking confirmations, reminders and alerts |
| Timesheets | Attendance and working-hour verification |
| Payments | Nurse payments and facility invoicing |
| Ratings | Nurse and facility feedback |
| Administration | Platform management, reporting and compliance |

## 5. Business Opportunity

The on-demand nursing marketplace model provides an alternative to traditional healthcare recruitment agencies.

The platform connects healthcare facilities directly with qualified nursing professionals, enabling flexible workforce management and reducing the administrative overhead associated with traditional staffing arrangements.
