# Democratization of AI
## UnityCloud AI Harness — Project Manifold

**The future of AI is local. The power to innovate should belong to everyone.**

Moore’s Law has demonstrated for decades that computing power continues to advance exponentially while the cost of compute decreases. Processing, networking and storage capabilities that once required enterprise data centres are now increasingly accessible to individuals and businesses.

The same transformation is happening with AI.

The idea that you need a supercomputer, an expensive GPU cluster or a hyperscale cloud subscription to run powerful AI is becoming increasingly outdated. Breakthroughs in model architecture, quantisation, inference optimisation and hardware efficiency are making increasingly capable AI accessible on affordable, locally deployed infrastructure.

Large technology companies understand this. Their business models are built around controlling infrastructure, platforms and access to AI. But the future of AI does not have to depend on centralised platforms, expensive subscriptions or proprietary ecosystems.

**At UnityCloud, we believe AI should be accessible, private and under your control.**

We want to help businesses take ownership of their AI capabilities, improve how they operate, deliver better services to their customers and become more competitive.

That is why we are building **Project Manifold**.

## More than an AI harness. A complete AI infrastructure.

Project Manifold is designed to be a complete, turnkey AI ecosystem built around open-source technology. It brings together everything required to deploy, operate, secure and manage your own AI environment.

Our vision is to remove the complexity of building an AI platform from the ground up, giving businesses a ready-to-use foundation that they can own, extend and customise.

With Project Manifold:

- **Your AI. Your infrastructure.** Deploy and operate AI locally, without depending on external AI providers.
- **Your data stays yours.** Keep sensitive business information, intellectual property and customer data within your own infrastructure.
- **Security by design.** Control access, authentication, permissions and how AI interacts with your systems and information.
- **Built on open source.** Leverage established open-source projects, contribute improvements back to the community and benefit from continuous innovation.
- **Complete customisation.** Adapt models, workflows, integrations and business processes to your specific requirements.
- **No vendor lock-in.** Maintain control over your technology, infrastructure and future direction.

## Take control of your AI future.

Businesses should not have to wait for software vendors to introduce features, change their pricing or decide which capabilities they can access.

With Project Manifold, you have the foundation to experiment, innovate and improve your business processes on your terms.

Build your own AI capabilities. Automate your operations. Protect your intellectual property. Deliver better customer experiences.

The democratisation of AI is not about giving everyone access to another chatbot. It is about giving businesses the infrastructure, tools and freedom to build AI around their own needs.

**The future of AI belongs to those who can build, adapt and innovate.**

**UnityCloud Project Manifold — Own your AI. Own your infrastructure. Own your future.**
