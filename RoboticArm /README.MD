# Wire suspended Robotic Arm [Remote controlled] - with different cleaning, vaccum, hand accessories 

'Robotic Arm  Suspended from the Kitchen Ceiling that claw fingers that can extend to clean the kitchen.'


Closest match — "Stringman": An open-source, ceiling-mounted robot built by engineer Nathaniel Nifong. It uses cables suspended from four corners of a room to move a robotic gripper across the ceiling, locating and collecting items like toys and clothing. The crane scours across the ceiling, identifies targets to pick up, and moves them to predesignated drop-off points. It's built to declutter rooms generally (not kitchen-specific), and the designs/build instructions are available on GitHub, with ready-made kits also offered.

Hackaday

Kitchen-specific ceiling arms exist too, but for cooking, not cleaning: Samsung showed off a "Bot Chef" concept where two white arms hang down from the ceiling, reaching across the table to chop, whisk, pour, or tidy up as a real chef works. That's more of a cooking assistant than a dedicated kitchen cleaner. 
Personal Robots

Adjacent commercial work: Companies like Armstrong Robotics are building systems with off-the-shelf robot arms and interchangeable fingers — including magnetic fingers for silverware — aimed at automating dishwashing in commercial kitchens, though that's floor-mounted, not ceiling-suspended. 
The Robot Report

So: no single product on the market today is exactly "a ceiling-suspended robotic arm with extending claw fingers that cleans your kitchen." But the Stringman project is a real, working, open-source version of almost that exact concept — just general-purpose rather than kitchen-specific. If you're picturing something more polished/consumer-ready, that still seems to be a few years off.
